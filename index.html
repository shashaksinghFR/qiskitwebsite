About this Event

  • Global Quantum Series — A worldwide, student-led quantum computing event series supported by IBM, bringing together learners and technology enthusiasts.
  • Introduction to Quantum Computing — Get introduced to the fundamentals of quantum computing and understand the ideas behind qubits, quantum states, and quantum circuits.
  • Open to Everyone — The event is open to students, faculty members, developers, researchers, and anyone interested in exploring quantum computing. No prior experience is required.
  • Explore Qiskit — Learn about IBM’s Qiskit framework and how it is used to create and experiment with quantum circuits.
  • Understand Real-World Applications — Discover how quantum computing can be applied to areas such as optimization, machine learning, and other emerging technologies.
  • Learn, Connect & Explore — Join us at Amity University Lucknow Campus to gain insights into this transformative technology and connect with others interested in the quantum future.
