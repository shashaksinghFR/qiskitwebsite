About this Event

Qiskit Fall Fest is a student-led, worldwide series of quantum computing events supported by IBM, bringing together students, developers, researchers, and technology enthusiasts from around the world. Through workshops, hackathons, challenges, and interactive sessions, the event aims to make quantum computing more accessible and inspire the next generation of quantum innovators. It provides participants with an opportunity to learn the fundamentals of quantum computing, explore Qiskit, build quantum circuits, and understand how quantum technologies can be applied to real-world problems.

Here at Amity University Lucknow Campus, we are bringing the cutting edge of Quantum Computing directly to our campus and creating a space where curiosity meets innovation. Amity University Lucknow is committed to fostering technology, research, entrepreneurship, and student-led innovation, making it an ideal platform for an event focused on one of the most transformative technologies of the future.

Join us to explore quantum fundamentals, participate in hands-on circuit building, tackle real-world optimization challenges, and connect with fellow students and quantum enthusiasts. Whether you are completely new to quantum computing or already experimenting with Qiskit, Qiskit Fall Fest at Amity University Lucknow is an opportunity to learn, collaborate, build, and take your first step into the quantum future.
