About this Event

Qiskit Fall Fest is a student-led, worldwide series of quantum computing events supported by IBM. It features workshops, hackathons, and challenges designed to grow the local quantum community and introduce students to Qiskit.

Here at Amity University, we are bringing the cutting edge of Quantum Computing directly to our campus. Join us to explore quantum fundamentals, engage in hands-on circuit building, and tackle real-world optimization problems alongside industry experts.
